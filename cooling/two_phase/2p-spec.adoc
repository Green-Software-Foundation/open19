== Connector mounting polarization

Cage manifolds shall have body (female) blind mate connectors. Bricks shall have insert (male) blind-mate connectors. When viewed from rear of the brick, vapor connection shall be located at the right position. Liquid connection shall be located at the left position.

== Brick panel detail

* The connectors on the brick side are panel mount with plastic hose connection
** Mate connection type: Blind mate (non-latched).
** G 1/4" male thread for the liquid side - needs to add a tube adaptor for 6mm OD tube (for example reference- PARKERS FEMALE STUD FITTING WITH BSPP FEMALE THREAD 6814 06 13)
** G 1/2" thread for the vapor side- needs to add a tube adaptor for 12mm OD tube (for example reference- PARKERS FEMALE STUD FITTING WITH BSPP FEMALE THREAD 6814 12 21)
* The connectors on the manifold side are parallel male thread
** Mate connection type: Blind mate (non-latched).
** G 1/4” for the liquid side
** G 1/2" for the vapor side

== Vapor connector ==

The vapor connector pressure drop influences the saturated coolant temperature. It causes a slight increase to saturation temperature and by that effects the cooling performances.

The CPC BLQ6 has a nominal diameter of 3/8 inch connector gives a max increase of 4.2°C at 3.5kW (0.24 bar pressure drop).

== Liquid connector ==

The liquid connector should supply max flow of 1 l/m to support the max power dissipation of 3.6 kW. The max recommended velocity of the system liquid lines is 2.5 m/s. This requires a Nominal diameter larger than 3mm.

The CPC BLQ4 Series connector has a nominal diameter of 1/4 inch that exceeds the above requirement.

= Tested example solution

Vapor connection:

The vapor connection type are two 1" Tri-clamp Ferrule connection. From the manifold side- the fittings are welded into the lower and upper parts of the manifold.

Vapor tubing- Shall be stainless steel corrugated tubes type.

Liquid connection:

The liquid connection shall be with a 1/2" Tri-clamp Ferrule connection. the left-lower part (looking from the back of the cage).

Liquid tubing: Shall be stainless steel corrugated tubes type.
