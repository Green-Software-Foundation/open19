== {UBrick} insertion and retention assistance for liquid cooling

For liquid cooling applications, two different mechanical assist for insertion and retention are available in the cage. {UBrick}s can implement features to make use either/both features.

The features are:

- {UBrick} retention standoff, available on both sides in the mechanical guide channel

- Slots, available on both sides of the cage wall
