= 8RU Cage Manifold

== Attachment points

The 8RU cage provides consistent attachment points for both single-phase and two-phase liquid cooling manifolds. Two PEM nuts are available in every brick location.

== Manifold

The liquid cooling manifold is not interoperable between single-phase and two-phase, by design. The bricks provide a blind-mate projection from the brick into the liquid cooling manifold.
